# Morning recovery
The coach uses the large avatar, recommendation, next step and expandable evidence. Sleep, HRV and resting pulse retain their seven-day measured charts. Yesterday's stress and maximum Body Battery have separate seven-day charts with prior 14-day medians; these are not current battery readings. Sleep details use the existing daily sleep summaries response: deep/light/REM/awake seconds, overall sleep score and average sleep stress only when valid and provided. Private raw_metrics stores only a normalized sleep detail object, never the source payload. Missing or partial stages do not produce a fabricated composition. The bar represents proportions, not a hypnogram. Sleep quality can corroborate a personal sleep concern after seven reference nights; no independent stage thresholds, no manufactured readiness score. Total training minutes across sports supplement running volume. No automatic training edits. Existing health sync backfills the last 16 calendar days on its normal schedule; older unavailable details stay missing. Secondary imports remain disabled until separately authorized.
